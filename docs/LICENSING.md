## Terminology

PixSeal should be described in current documentation as **source-available** or
**source-available for noncommercial use**. It should not be described as
"open source" in the OSI sense, because the noncommercial restriction is a
field-of-use restriction and PolyForm Noncommercial is not OSI-approved.

This terminology change describes the licensing model only. It does not change
PixSeal's cryptographic, watermarking, interoperability or qualification
status.

## Historical MIT releases

PixSeal versions and copies that were already distributed under the MIT License
remain usable under the MIT grant that accompanied those copies. Changing the
license for later distributions does not revoke rights already granted under
MIT.

The current PolyForm license therefore applies prospectively to this source
distribution and later distributions that carry the same license. It does not
retroactively convert an already received MIT-licensed copy into a
noncommercial copy.

Relicensing also assumes that the project has the necessary rights to relicense
all code included in the affected distribution. If code from an external
copyright holder was accepted under terms that require preservation of another
license or notice, those obligations remain applicable to that code.

## Redistribution requirements

When redistributing PixSeal under the current license, keep either the complete
PolyForm terms or the official license URL with the software, and preserve all
plain-text lines beginning with `Required Notice:` supplied by the project.
The repository therefore ships both `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`, and source/release
packaging should retain both files.

## Commercial licensing

The PolyForm Noncommercial license intentionally does not grant commercial-use
rights. Commercial licensing can be offered separately by the applicable
copyright holder(s) without changing the noncommercial public distribution.

The repository does not define commercial pricing or business terms. Those
terms, if any, belong in a separate commercial agreement rather than in the
PolyForm license text.

## Private corpora and acquisition material

The source-code license does **not** imply that PixSeal's private qualification
corpora, photographs, scans, generated private fixtures or other private
experimental material are distributed or licensed with the source tree.

Directories such as `original pics/`, `print-camera private/`, `print-scan
private/`, `private-fixtures/`, `v4-physical private/` and `v4-phone private/`
remain private research material and are excluded from source/release archives.
Unless a separate notice explicitly says otherwise, do not redistribute those
files as part of PixSeal.
